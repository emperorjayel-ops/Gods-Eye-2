# HIIYAH eSIM — Go-live checklist (in order)

1. [ ] White-label / MVNO platform partner signed — SM-DP+ address, test profiles, API docs, pricing
2. [ ] Test activation on a physical iPhone (iOS 17.4+) and a physical Android (API 28+) with test EIDs only
3. [ ] Apple Developer (organisation) + Google Play developer accounts, in HIIYAH's name, signing keys secured
4. [ ] Privacy policy + terms hosted, support contact live, store listing + screenshots completed
5. [ ] Billing, KYC, taxes, and telecom compliance reviewed for each market sold in
6. [ ] Submit to App Store + Play, respond to review questions with partner details (do not claim arbitrary provisioning)
7. [ ] Launch with one market / plan first, then expand — do not launch all markets at once
